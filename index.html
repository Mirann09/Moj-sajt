04·ProcessKako radim

Four steps, from first message to launch.Četiri koraka, od prve poruke do objave.

  1. 01

    Free review and a short callBesplatna analiza i kratak razgovor

    Send me your site or your Instagram. Within 24 hours I send you what I'd change, then we have a short call about what you actually need.Pošalješ mi link sajta ili Instagram profila. Za 24 sata ti pošaljem šta bih promenio, pa se kratko čujemo da vidimo šta ti stvarno treba.

  2. 02

    Plan and designPlan i dizajn

    We agree on pages, content and a deadline, in writing. Then I design it, so you see how the site will look before I build anything.Dogovorimo stranice, sadržaj i rok, napismeno. Onda dizajniram, i ti vidiš kako će sajt izgledati pre nego što počnem da ga pravim.

  3. 03

    Build and test on phonesIzrada i testiranje na telefonu

    I build the site and test it on phones, tablets and desktops. Phones come first, because that's where most people will find you.Pravim sajt i proveravam ga na telefonu, tabletu i računaru. Telefon proveravam prvi, jer te ljudi najčešće tamo traže.

  4. 04

    Launch and handoverObjava i predaja

    I set up the domain and hosting for you, you just pay for them. Then I launch and hand over every login. Small fixes in the first 30 days are on me.Domen i hosting podesim ja, ti ih samo platiš. Objavim sajt i predam ti sve pristupe. Sitne izmene prvih 30 dana su besplatne.

06·AboutO meni

Hi, I'm Mirko.Ćao, ja sam Mirko.

I started from zero about two years ago, and since then I've been building websites for small and mid-sized businesses, from the first call to launch. I work remotely from Apatin (a small town on the Danube, in Serbia), with clients in Serbia, the EU and the US. Most of my work happens in Webflow, but the project decides the tool. I know HTML and CSS, I'm learning JavaScript, and I connect forms and emails with Make. You deal with me directly the whole way, nobody in between. Next on my list is vibe coding: building tools with AI as a coding partner.

Pre oko dve godine sam krenuo od nule, i od tada pravim sajtove za male i srednje biznise, od prvog razgovora do objave. Radim na daljinu iz Apatina (mali grad na Dunavu), sa klijentima iz Srbije, EU i SAD. Najviše radim u Webflow-u, ali alat biram prema tome šta projekat traži. Znam HTML i CSS, sad učim JavaScript, a forme i mejlove povezujem preko Make-a. Sve vreme radiš direktno sa mnom, bez posrednika. Sledeće na listi mi je vibe coding, pravljenje alata uz AI.
